

🏠 EstateIQ

AI-Powered Real Estate Decision Intelligence Platform

«From Raw Property Data to Evidence-Grounded Investment Decisions.»

EstateIQ is an end-to-end Real Estate Decision Intelligence Platform designed to answer one core business question:

Should I INVEST, HOLD, or AVOID this real-estate opportunity?

The platform combines data engineering, SQL analytics, statistical intelligence, predictive analytics, evidence-grounded RAG, financial modeling, Monte Carlo risk analysis, geo-spatial intelligence, multi-agent orchestration, Power BI decision experiences, and governed AI explanations into one integrated decision system.

---

🚀 What Makes EstateIQ Different?

EstateIQ is not just a dashboard, ML model, or chatbot.

It creates a complete decision pipeline:

Trusted Data → Business Intelligence → Statistical Intelligence → Predictive Intelligence → Evidence Intelligence → Financial Intelligence → Risk Intelligence → Geo Intelligence → Multi-Agent Decision Intelligence → Decision Experience → AI Explanation

The system is designed around three principles:

- Evidence before conclusions
- Deterministic calculations for decision-critical numbers
- No silent guessing when data is insufficient

🔹 Phase 4 — Predictive & Advanced Analytics

Builds and evaluates predictive models while preserving model governance.

Predictive areas

- Property valuation
- Rent prediction
- Price forecasting
- Demand forecasting
- Days-on-market
- Sale probability

Governance

Models are classified using explicit states such as:

PASS · PASS WITH LIMITATIONS · CONDITIONAL · REJECTED · UNCALIBRATED

Rejected or weak models are not silently presented as reliable predictions.

EstateIQ follows a No Silent Guessing philosophy.

Standard decision states

PASS

CONDITIONAL
REJECTED
BLOCKED
INSUFFICIENT DATA
UNCALIBRATED

⚠️ Important Limitations

EstateIQ is an advanced decision-intelligence platform and portfolio/engineering project, not a guarantee of real-world investment returns.

Important limitations include:

- Synthetic or limited datasets where applicable
- Model calibration limitations
- Evidence coverage limitations
- Data availability constraints
- Simulation assumptions
- Geo-data limitations
- Real-world market uncertainty

Where sufficient evidence is unavailable, the system is designed to communicate INSUFFICIENT DATA rather than inventing an answer.
